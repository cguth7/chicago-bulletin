The ledger — why a quiet venue still counts

Filed once, then kept honest

Venues outlive any single week. The directory keeps the room even when the room has nothing on, because that is the truthful state:

  • Filed — a venue enters the ledger the first time a lead or an event names it
  • Resolved — every event lands against one venue record, never a duplicate
  • Placed — a pin gets a neighborhood from the city's boundaries, not from a guess
  • Live — live means a built pipeline is watching, not that tonight is busy
